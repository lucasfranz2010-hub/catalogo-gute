## Brand & Style

This design system translates the tactile sensuality, sculptural precision, and organic warmth of contemporary high jewelry and artisanal leathercraft into an immersive digital environment. The brand personality balances quiet luxury, museum-grade curation, and grounded warmth. It rejects cold, clinical minimalism in favor of an artisanal tactile editorial style—where natural textures, buttery warm whites, deep cognac leathers, and molten gold hardware create an intimate, tactile resonance.

The target audience comprises discerning collectors, design connoisseurs, and contemporary luxury patrons who value authorial voice, craftsmanship, and sculptured form over ostentatious branding. The interface evokes contemplation, tactile desire, and effortless serenity through deliberate whitespace, slow-paced visual rhythm, and refined typography.

## Colors

The palette directly references raw organic materials: rich vegetable-tanned leather, polished vermeil gold, and unbleached Belgian linen.

- **Primary (`#5A331E`)**: Deep Cognac / Chocolate Leather. Serves as the principal anchor for high-contrast typography, primary interactive states, and core architectural frames.
- **Secondary (`#C69C4E`)**: Brushed Artisan Gold. Evokes metallic hardware, brand emblems, and precise luxury accents; used sparingly for collection seals, key focal points, and active indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#8C5835`)**: Warm Caramel Umber. Provides mid-tone depth for hover transitions, secondary buttons, subheadings, and subtle border dividers.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2`)**: Off-White Silk Linen. Creates a welcoming, warm foundation that softens eye fatigue compared to sterile pure white.
- **Supporting Surface Tone (`#F5EFEB`)**: Warm Alabaster / Buttermilk. Applied to elevated card containers, input fields, and modal backdrops to establish soft, physical layering.

## Typography

The typographical treatment reflects an editorial balance between classical high-fashion tradition and modern ergonomic usability. 

- **Display & Headings (`Playfair Display`)**: Employs elegant transitional serif letterforms with delicate stroke contrast. Headings should be styled predominantly in regular weight (`400`) to evoke literary grace and editorial pedigree rather than aggressive weight.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers crisp geometric clarity with subtle humanist warmth. Its modern, rounded aperatures make micro-labels, pricing, and narrative product descriptions exceptionally legible on digital screens.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Employs uppercase setting with tracking expanded between `0.08em` and `0.14em` to simulate luxury blind-embossing and hallmarks.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an expansive, fluid 12-column grid system designed around intentional breathing room. The architecture avoids dense data packing, letting individual pieces occupy prominent negative space.

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (320px–767px, 4-column flow), Tablet (768px–1024px, 8-column flow), Desktop (1025px+, 12-column flow with a maximum constrained content container of 1440px).
- **Asymmetric Offsets**: Editorial narrative rows alternate between centered gallery columns and off-axis dual columns to mimic art book monographs.
- **Rhythm**: Component interiors maintain strict multiples of 4px and 8px, prioritizing wide internal card padding (`space-xl` on desktop) to let jewelry imagery float effortlessly.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids generic drop shadows, utilizing warm ambient light scattering and tonal surface progression.

- **Tonal Stepping**: The foundation begins on neutral canvas `#FAF7F2`. Elevated surfaces, cards, and drawers sit on `#F5EFEB`, framed by a hairlike border (`1px solid rgba(90, 51, 30, 0.08)`).
- **Amber Ambient Shadows**: Raised elements cast long, extra-diffused shadows tinted with warm cognac (`rgba(90, 51, 30, 0.05)` to `rgba(90, 51, 30, 0.08)`) with blur radiuses spanning 24px to 48px. This recreates natural studio spotlighting rather than synthetic grey blurs.
- **Floating Overlays & Drawers**: Modals and quick-view panels employ a backdrop filter (`backdrop-blur: 12px`) with a 60% translucent warm alabaster wash (`rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.85)`).

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette reflects the tailored curvature of luxury leather goods—neither rigidly razor-sharp nor overly playful. 

- Baseline elements (buttons, inputs, standard cards) feature soft curvature (`rounded-md`, 4px–6px), echoing fine saddle stitching and pressed corners.
- Large editorial display cards, modal sheets, and collection frames leverage `rounded-lg` (8px–12px) to soften transitions against the expansive layout.
- Badges and metallic seals adopt fully organic pill curvatures (`rounded-full`) to reference jewelry hardware and stamped logo tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Deep cognac background (`#5A331E`), off-white typography (`#FAF7F2`), subtle uppercase letter-spacing (`label-md`). Hover elevates subtly with a shift toward `#8C5835` accompanied by a soft cognac glow.
- **Secondary Action**: Bordered button featuring a 1px solid stroke in `#5A331E` or `#C69C4E`, transparent background, and deep cognac text. Hover fills the container with `rgba(198, 156, 78, 0.08)`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Text-only with an animated 1px underline expanding from center in gold `#C69C4E`.

### Cards & Product Displays
- Built on `#F5EFEB` with a 1px border (`rgba(90, 51, 30, 0.06)`). Product images rest on neutral, warm-lit studio backdrops.
- Product titles utilize `headline-sm` with price points rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans Medium`. On hover, the image performs a subtle 1.02x scale zoom with smooth easing (cubic-bezier 0.25, 1, 0.5, 1).

### Collection Badges & Tags
- **Artisanal Hallmark Badge**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), rendered with a light metallic gold tint background (`rgba(198, 156, 78, 0.12)`), gold text (`#A17728`), and an ultra-fine 1px border (`#C69C4E`).
- **Material Pill**: Minimalist badge in linen white with cognac text indicating leather origin, gold purity, or limited edition status.

### Form Inputs
- Field backgrounds adopt `#FAF7F2` with bottom or full subtle perimeter borders (`1px solid rgba(90, 51, 30, 0.15)`).
- Focused states transition the border to `#C69C4E` without jarring outlines, supported by a 2px diffuse ring (`rgba(198, 156, 78, 0.15)`).
- Floating labels transform using `label-sm` in uppercase tracking.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Custom squared rings with 3px border radius in `#5A331E`. When checked, the box fills with `#5A331E` displaying a gold `#C69C4E` checkmark.
- Radio toggles use concentric rings: outer cognac frame with an inner solid gold medallion.

### Spec Sheets & Accordions
- Clean horizontal rules (`1px solid rgba(90, 51, 30, 0.1)`) separating artisanal details (leather grain, hardware material, dimensions). Chevron triggers rotate smoothly with 300ms easing.
